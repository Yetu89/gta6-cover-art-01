Vice Sunset Cover Lab is an offline static web app that turns a photo and a caption into GTA 6-style poster cover art. It offers six layouts — full-bleed hero, hero plus three, a four-panel grid, a nine-panel grid with the title in the centre cell, tilted polaroid snapshots on a sunset, and a split story — plus five gradient-map colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Gold, Ocean Drive, and Mono Noir) with film grain and vignette. Each photo can be cropped, panned, zoomed, and tuned for brightness, contrast, saturation, and blur; the title is editable in Anton, Pacifico, or bold sans with a neon-script subtitle, caption presets and a 1–5 star heat meter round out the poster, and undo/redo, autosave, and a dark/light theme keep the workflow smooth. Finished covers export as PNG in three post sizes: 1080x1080, 1080x1350, and 1920x1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
